Burning desire

To build a company that ignites people's ambition.

Japanese manufacturing is full of technologies that are still little known, and of people whose passion sustains them. I want to communicate that value and create more encounters with the companies and researchers who need it. Drawing on my experience in sales and business development, and on the relationships I have built, I want to be a force for people and companies who take on challenges.

Being happy together

My real treasures are the bonds and memories I share with the people who raised me and spent time with me. When I achieve something big, I want as many of them as possible by my side. And if you, reading this, become one of them, nothing would make me happier.

Things nobody knows about me

I hold dan ranks in kendo and Nippon Kempo, and a Grade 1 aromatherapy certificate. I have served in the Japan Self-Defense Forces and worked part-time at Universal Studios Japan.

At university I studied molecular neurobiology and educational technology; my interest in the body, the mind and how people learn still shapes my work today.

I have a serious sweet tooth. I also love ghost stories, yokai, cryptids and everything else filed under "the occult".

I am not the toughest mentally, so I keep myself steady by looking through old photos and soaking in the memories.

My keys to success

Knowing the front line, listening to people, and turning ideas into things that actually work.

I connect what I learned in manufacturing, IT and education, and think everything through from defining the problem to sales strategy and building the business machinery. I am grateful for every relationship and aim to answer people's expectations through my work.

I try to treat everyone involved with respect, and to take pride in my own work and experience (or at least to live each day in a way that lets me).

Running my own business also means meeting no small number of dishonest, arrogant, unsavoury or dangerous propositions and people. I apologise in advance, but I take a firm stance against anything of that kind.

My strengths & who to refer

USP

I have visited more than 2,500 manufacturing sites. At KEYENCE I ranked No. 1 nationwide in non-contact measurement sales. Getting told off by craftsmen and company presidents on the shop floor, I learned first-hand how to read a technology and how to communicate its value so it lands.

I then worked in enterprise customer success at AWS and business development at Recruit. In parallel I joined the management of six startups as executive or department head, building companies and sales systems across very different fields: manufacturing DX, inbound media, an art gallery, a listed-company subsidiary and more.

Knowing three front lines, manufacturing, IT and education, and thinking not only about what is excellent but about who to tell and in what words. That is my strength in running TAKUMI, communicating companies' technologies and initiatives, and widening their encounters with manufacturers, service providers, practitioners at major makers, and universities and research institutes.
